Getting there

The Void is reached through a 5 × 8 portal frame. The four corners are Crying Frames; every other frame block is Crying Obsidian. The inside is 3 wide and 6 tall.

  1. Build the frame standing up, like a Nether portal.
  2. Right-click each corner Crying Frame with a Void Shard. The corner lights up and the shard is used.
  3. When all four corners are lit, the portal opens.

Biomes

The sky is always purple and it is always day. Islands float over the void, linked by bridges that climb and dip. Six biomes:

Void Forest

Dense woods of Void Logs and glowing Void Leaves.

Shadow Grove

Darker, quieter woodland with scattered trees.

Crying Wastes

Wide, open islands with few trees.

Void Mire

Low, wet ground with pools of Void Water.

Voiddessert

Dry, bare islands with almost no plants.

Void Highlands

Tall, uneven mountains with lakes and waterfalls of Void Water.

Structures

  • Void bridges connect islands, with ramps, arches, dips and broken sections.
  • Floating islands: forest, spires, ruins, ponds, desert and clusters.
  • Void Sanctuary: a rare walled arena with a moat. The Void Sovereign lives here, next to a loot chest.

The Void Sovereign

Find him in the Void Sanctuary. He has a purple boss bar and seven attacks. Below half health he enters phase 2: he roars, sends out a shockwave, gains Speed and Strength, attacks more often and surrounds himself with fangs.

  1. Void SlamLeaps and crashes down with a shockwave around him.
  2. Fang LineA row of evoker fangs bursts toward you.
  3. Void RiftsPurple circles appear under you and explode upward after a moment. Keep moving.
  4. Void BeamCharges, then fires a beam. Break line of sight to dodge it.
  5. Shadow StepTeleports behind you and strikes.
  6. Gravity WellPulls everyone in and applies Darkness and Slowness.
  7. SummonPhase 2 only: calls two Voidbeasts.

Void Water & Void Corruption

The lakes, pools and waterfalls of the Void are Void Water. Touching it gives you Void Corruption for 5 seconds. Pick it up with an empty bucket to get a Void Water Bucket.

  • Drains your health slowly, but never below 1 heart point. It can't kill you on its own.
  • Makes you hungry.
  • At level II or higher, it randomly blinks you a short distance.
  • Immune: mobs from this mod and players wearing the full Void armor set.

FAQ

Client or server?

Both. Install it on the server and on every player's game. In single player, just install it in your game.

Which loader?

Fabric (needs Fabric API) or NeoForge, for Minecraft 26.1.2 and 26.3. Download the file that matches your loader and version.

Can I sleep in the Void?

No. Beds can't be used there, and it is always day anyway.

My portal won't open

Check the size (5 wide, 8 tall, outside measurement), that the four corners are Crying Frames, that the rest is Crying Obsidian, and that the inside is empty air.
